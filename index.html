How I work

Instrument the problem before you style the screen

Most of the dashboards I inherit are hard to use because they were built to show what the underlying system produces, not what the person watching it needs to decide. I start every project by mapping the decisions someone has to make and the minimum information that supports each one — the interface follows from that, not the other way round.

Concretely, that means sitting with the people who'll use the tool at the hours they actually use it, prototyping in the messiest real data I can get, and testing legibility under time pressure rather than in a calm design review.
