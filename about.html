About

I design the instruments people use to run complicated things.

I'm a product and interaction designer with nine years of experience, currently taking on select freelance and contract engagements from Bengaluru. Before going independent, I led design for internal tools at two infrastructure companies.

My background is half visual design, half systems thinking — I trained as a graphic designer, then spent three years pairing with backend engineers on observability tooling, which is where the current focus on data-dense interfaces came from. I care most about the moment a dashboard actually gets used under pressure: a 2am incident, a closing deadline, a truck stuck without signal. Those constraints, not visual trends, are what shape the work.
